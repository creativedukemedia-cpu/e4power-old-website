BRIDGE

The BRIDGE program (Building Research Initiatives & Developing Growth in Economics) aims to enhance research initiatives through collaborative evaluations and feedback. The program fosters continuous improvement and sustainability, guiding researchers in identifying future funding opportunities. Ultimately, BRIDGE creates an environment where innovative research flourishes and educational initiatives thrives throughout the funding lifecycle.
